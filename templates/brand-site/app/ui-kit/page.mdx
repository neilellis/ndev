## How it is put together

| Band | Surface | Parts |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Paper | Eyebrow, display heading with the signature move, intro, one dark button, one text link |
| {{product}} | Navy | Section top, section heading |
| Services | Paper | Section top, section heading, service columns |
| Contact | Paper | Eyebrow, heading, one dark button |

The website puts Experience, Ideas & insights and About us between Services and Contact. See [Page sections](/sections). Each service column above shows two of its four items. The full lists are on the website.

## Start a new page

Link the design system stylesheet and wrap the page in `.ds`:

```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="{{siteUrl}}/ds/styles.css">
<body class="ds">
  <!-- Copy the bands above. Keep one dark button per view. -->
</body>
```
