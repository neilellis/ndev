## Drawing rules

- Lines 1–2px in navy or hairline grey. No 3D, no gradients inside shapes, no drop shadows on diagram parts.
- Label directly on the diagram in Source Sans. Avoid separate keys when a label fits.
- Infographics must come from published material (the website or the essays). Credit them as “{{name}}, from the essays”.
