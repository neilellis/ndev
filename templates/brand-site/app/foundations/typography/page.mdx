# Typography

Two Adobe open-source families, self-hosted: **Source Sans 3** for everything and **Source Serif 4** for emphasis. Both are in `/ds/assets/fonts/` with their licences.

## The signature

Headings are set in Source Sans at regular weight with tight negative tracking. The emphasised phrase switches to Source Serif italic in blue. That contrast is the brand's most recognisable typographic move.

## Rules

- Headings are weight 400. Use 600 only for buttons, links, eyebrows, labels and the nav.
- Line height is 1.12 for headings and 1.6 for body.
- Keep body lines to about 70 characters.
- Use the serif for italic emphasis, numbers in ruled lists (`01`, `02`), article titles and quotes.
- Never use the serif in buttons, labels or captions.
- Sentence case everywhere. Capitals only for eyebrows and small labels, through CSS.

## Eyebrows

Numbered eyebrows open each home-page section. The number is blue and set apart by a slash.

| Body text | 26px minimum |
