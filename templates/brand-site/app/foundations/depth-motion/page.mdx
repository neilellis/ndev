# Depth and motion

The website is flat. Rules and tinted bands separate content. Shadows appear only on things that float above the page, and motion is short and quiet.

## Web motion

- Hover and state changes: 160–200ms, `ease-out`.
- The globe rotates slowly and stops on hover, focus or when a concept is selected.
- A visible motion toggle pauses movement. `prefers-reduced-motion` stops it by default.
- No parallax, scroll-jacking or entrance animations on scroll.

See [Animation principles](/animation/principles) and [Animation patterns](/animation/patterns) for the full rules.

## Film motion

Film motion is calmer than most corporate video. See [Motion](/video/motion) for the full rules.

| Movement | Value |
|---|---|
| Element entrance | 0.7s, `power3.out`, rising 42px (18px for images) |
| Scene cross-fade | 0.55s, `power1.inOut` |
| Ken Burns on photographs | scale 1 → 1.09 across the scene, linear |
| Compass needle | swings from −200° to 0° over 1.6s |
