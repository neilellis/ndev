## Rules

- Write the name as **{{name}}**, exactly, every time. Never change its case, spacing or punctuation.
- Leave clear space around the wordmark of at least the height of its capital letters.
- Minimum width: 96px on screen.
- Do not stretch, outline, recolour, add effects or redraw the mark.
- The wordmark is in `app/components/Wordmark.tsx`. The mark is `/ds/assets/mark.svg`.

## In film

On the brand and end-card scenes, the mark animates into place over 1.6s with `power3.out`. The rest of the wordmark stays still.
