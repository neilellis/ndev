# Colour

A restrained palette: navy ink on cool paper, with a single blue accent. Most screens are 90% paper and navy. Blue is used sparingly, so it marks what matters.

## Concept families

The website's globe and the film's network diagram sort ideas into two families. These colours carry that meaning and nothing else. Always pair them with a second cue, a shape or a label, so the meaning survives without colour.

## Gradients

Gradients come from the film. Use them for full-bleed dark scenes, end cards and soft light backgrounds. Never put a gradient on a button or behind body text in a card.
