# Core principles

The short version. If you are an AI tool producing {{name}} work, read this page in full before you start, then check the detailed page for anything you make.

## Look

1. **Navy, paper and one blue.** Ink is navy `#102846`, the canvas is paper `#f6f8fa`, and the single accent is blue `#2166ac`. On navy, the accent becomes sky `#a7d8ff`. Do not introduce other accent colours.
2. **Green and orange mean something.** Forest `#173f35` marks organisation concepts; rust `#a94f24` marks AI concepts. Use them only for that distinction, never for decoration.
3. **Two type families.** Source Sans for everything; Source Serif for italic emphasis, numbers in lists and article titles. Headings are regular weight (400) with tight negative tracking. Never bold a heading.
4. **The signature move** is a plain statement followed by a serif italic phrase in blue: “Strategic insight. *Lasting impact.*” Use it once per view.
5. **Flat and ruled.** Separate content with 1px hairline rules and tinted bands, not boxes and shadows. Corners are near-square (2px). Shadows are for things that float.
6. **Generous space.** Sections breathe: 90px padding on desktop, a 1320px content width, wide gutters.

## Voice

7. **British English, sentence case, plain words.** Short sentences. Specific headings. No hype, no buzzwords, no exclamation marks.
8. **Evidence first.** Every claim must be supported by the website or the published essays. Label what is told, shown or measured.
9. **No banned words.** Check every text against the [banned words](/copy/banned). Choose one of the [five registers](/copy) first. Documentation, technical documentation and agent text use [simple English](/copy/simple-english): “must”, not “should”, and no contractions.
10. **The name is {{name}}.** Write it exactly, every time. Spoken as “{{nameSpoken}}”.

## Film

11. **1920×1080 at 30fps, captions burned in.** It must work with the sound off.
12. **Alternate light and dark scenes.** Photography under a navy scrim, clean white scenes and navy-to-blue gradients.
13. **Calm motion.** Elements ease in on the spoken word. No bounces, spins, glitches, lens flares or particle effects.
14. **Real, credited photography.** Real places and modern workplaces in calm light. No glowing brains, robots, binary code or staged handshakes. Credit every photographer.
15. **Narration:** a deep, calm British voice (OpenAI `onyx`), at a measured pace. Numbers and the web address written as words in the script.

## Process

16. Use the tokens in `/ds/tokens/`, not raw hex values, when you can.
17. Check text contrast against [Accessibility](/accessibility). Honour reduced motion. Every page must work at 390px, with touch targets of at least 44px. See [Responsive design](/responsive).
18. Show your work as a preview and get approval before anything is published.
