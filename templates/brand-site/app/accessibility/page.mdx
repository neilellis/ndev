# Accessibility

The website aims for WCAG 2.2 AA. The same standard applies to documents and film.

## Contrast

Calculated from the token values when this site is built.

<ContrastTable />

- Body text needs at least 4.5:1. Large text (24px, or 19px bold) needs 3:1.
- Faint `#6b8095` is for decorative labels such as map coordinates, never for information.
- Forest and rust pass for text on white, but always pair them with a shape or label.

## Interaction

- Focus is always visible: a 3px `--ring` outline with a 5px offset.
- Every interactive element works with a keyboard. The globe supports arrow keys, Home and Escape.
- Provide a skip link to the main content.
- Touch targets are at least 44px. See [Input and touch](/ux/input-and-touch).
- Use semantic HTML: real buttons, links, headings and lists.
- Give every icon-only button an accessible name. The feedback button has `aria-label="Leave us a message"`.
- Every gesture has a single-tap or single-click alternative.
- No time limits. Nothing closes or moves on before the reader is ready.
- Never use colour alone. Pair it with a word, a shape or a position.
- Error messages say what went wrong and how to fix it. See [Feedback and performance](/ux/feedback-and-performance).

## Motion

- Honour `prefers-reduced-motion`: stop rotation and entrance animation.
- Give a visible control to pause anything that moves for more than five seconds.

## Images and film

- Describe images in alt text. Decorative images get empty alt text.
- Say when a screenshot shows fictional demonstration data.
- Film has burned-in captions and must make sense with the sound off. Keep a plain-text script (`script.md`) as a transcript.
- No flashing content. Nothing flashes more than three times a second.
