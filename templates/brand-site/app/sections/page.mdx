# Page sections

The home page is a sequence of full-width bands. Each opens with a numbered eyebrow and alternates surface so the reader feels the rhythm without boxes.

| Order | Section | Surface | Pattern |
|---|---|---|---|
| — | Hero | Paper | Display heading, intro, one dark button, a text link, and the feature diagram |
| 01 | Introducing {{product}} | Navy | Section heading and a tabbed product tour with screenshots on sand |
| 02 | Our services | Paper | Section heading and three ruled columns |
| — | Experience | Mist | Dated rows and client logos |
| 03 | Ideas & insights | Paper | Featured essay beside a numbered series list |
| 04 | About us | Mist | Story and a serif pull quote |
| 05 | Let’s talk | Paper | One clear action: book a conversation |
| — | Footer | Paper | A hairline rule above the wordmark, legal line and links |

## Rhythm

- Never put two navy bands next to each other.
- Mist bands use `--border-on-tint` rules, not the paper hairline.
- Keep section padding at 90px (58px on phones), whatever the content.
