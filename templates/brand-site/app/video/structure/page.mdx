## Rhythm

- **Alternate light and dark.** Dark: photograph or gradient. Light: white or paper-to-sky. Never run two dark scenes back to back.
- **One idea per scene.** One headline, one supporting visual.
- **Scenes last as long as their narration.** Usually 3.5–11s. Do not hold a scene in silence, except the end card.
- **Open with a question, close with one action.**

## Timing rules

The timeline is built from the voice, not the other way round.

| Setting | Value | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `TEMPO` | 1.12 | Narration is sped up by 12%. Above about 1.18 it sounds rushed |
| `FIRST_LEAD` | 0.6s | Silence before the first word |
| `LEAD` | 0.25s | Silence before each later scene’s speech |
| `TAIL` | 0.4s | Hold after each scene’s speech |
| `LAST_TAIL` | 2.0s | Hold after the final line |
| `POST_WAIT` | 30s | End card stays up after the narration |
| `CREDITS_HOLD` | 8s | Credits page duration |
| Cross-fade | 0.55s | Between scenes |

## Variants

- **Social cut (about 30s):** scenes 0, 1, 2, 4 and 8. Keep the end card for at least 3s.
- **Square or vertical:** re-lay each scene for the frame; never crop the 16:9 render. Keep captions clear of platform buttons, so raise them to the lower third.
- **No-narration loop for events:** headlines and captions only, each scene about 5s.

Treat these as starting points. Each cut still needs its own script and review.
