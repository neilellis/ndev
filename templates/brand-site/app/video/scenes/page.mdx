# Scenes and look

Every scene uses one of three backgrounds: photography under a navy scrim, clean white, or a soft brand gradient. The frame has generous margins: content starts 120–140px from the left edge.

## Photography under a scrim

<Still src="/ds/assets/placeholder.svg" alt="City photograph with a navy scrim on the left and a large white headline" caption="Placeholder. Was: The hook. Text sits on the darkest side of the scrim." wide />

- Scrim: `--scrim-photo-left`, plus `--scrim-photo-base` along the bottom so captions stay legible.
- The headline sits on the left, about 1000px wide, starting 140px in.
- The photograph drifts slowly (Ken Burns).

## White

<Still src="/ds/assets/placeholder.svg" alt="Three white cards with blue top borders for Data, AI and Transformation" caption="Placeholder. Was: Service cards on white." wide />

**Film cards** are white with a 1px `--slate-200` border, a 6px blue top border, a 4px radius and `--elevation-card-film`. Inside: a 20px tracked number, a 50px title and 25px list items divided by faint rules.

## Product screenshots

- Show the real product. {{product}} screenshots show Example Corp, a fictional demonstration organisation; the credits page says so.
- An 8px radius, sand `#f4f1e9` backing and `--elevation-shot-film`.
- A navy tag in the top-left corner names the view: 20px semibold capitals.
- Principle chips beside the screenshot: white at 85%, a 5px blue left border, a tracked blue label and a 34px serif value.

## Dark scenes

Mark a dark scene with the `.dark` class. Headlines turn white, emphasis turns sky `#a7d8ff`, eyebrows turn `#c4d5e6`, and the wordmark switches to its on-navy colours.
