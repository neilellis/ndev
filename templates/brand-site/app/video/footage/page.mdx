## Unsplash routine

1. Search for real places, modern workplaces and calm light. See [Photography](/foundations/photography).
2. Before downloading, call the photo’s `download_location` endpoint. Unsplash’s API terms require it.
3. Download at `w=2400` and save as `u-<photo id>.jpg`.
4. Check the photo still reads under the scene’s scrim, with clear space for the headline.
5. Record the credit (below) in the same change.

## Credits, every time

1. Add a row to the credits file with the file, the photographer and the photo’s Unsplash page.
2. Add the photographer’s name to the credits page in the film, kept on one line.
3. When a photo is removed, remove its file, its row and its name.

Never use a photo without a recorded photographer.

## AI-generated imagery

The current film uses no AI-generated imagery or footage. If a future film does:

- Generate settings and atmosphere only. Never generate people presented as real staff or clients, client logos, or product screens.
- Say so on the credits page, naming the tool.
- Review each generated shot against [Photography](/foundations/photography) as if it were stock.
