# Review checklist

Check every film, and every generated shot, against this list before anyone outside {{name}} sees it. Do not describe a render you have not watched as checked.

## Content

- [ ] Every claim is supported by {{domain}} or the published essays.
- [ ] Client names appear only as “our team has worked with”.
- [ ] Screenshots are labelled as Meridian Grid, a fictional demonstration organisation.
- [ ] The name is written {{name}} and spoken “{{nameSpoken}}”.
- [ ] British spelling everywhere, in headlines and captions.
- [ ] One clear action at the end: book a conversation at {{domain}}.

## Look

- [ ] Brand colours only; green and orange only for the organisation and AI families.
- [ ] Light and dark scenes alternate.
- [ ] No text overlaps other text or is clipped at the edge.
- [ ] Nothing sits in the bottom 150px behind the captions.
- [ ] Photographs have a scrim wherever text sits on them.
- [ ] No AI clichés, staged handshakes or readable third-party text.

## Captions and sound

- [ ] Captions match the narration and what is on screen.
- [ ] No caption has more than seven words or is a single stranded word.
- [ ] Every element appears when its word is spoken, not before.
- [ ] Narration is clear, unhurried and correctly pronounced.
- [ ] Loudness is about −16dB mean with no clipping.

## File

- [ ] 1920×1080, 30fps, H.264 with AAC.
- [ ] Duration matches the timeline, including the end-card hold and credits.
- [ ] Frames taken from the MP4 itself look right. This catches encoding faults that stills miss.

## Credits

- [ ] Every photographer is named on the credits page and in the credits file.
- [ ] The generated voice and any generated imagery are disclosed.
- [ ] Every removed photo is also removed from the credits.

## Hand-off

Share the contact sheet and the MP4. In one or two sentences, say what changed, the new length, and anything you did not check.
