# Generation prompts

Ready-to-use prompts for AI video, image and voice tools. Paste the brand block first, then the shot or scene prompt. Change the bracketed parts only.

<Rule>
Generated material must still follow this guide. Review every output against [Photography](/foundations/photography) and the [review checklist](/video/review). Credit the tool on the credits page.
</Rule>

## Brand block

Paste this at the start of any prompt for a generative tool.

```text
Brand: {{name}}, a consultancy in technology, data and organisational transformation.
Audience: senior leaders in large organisations and public institutions.
Mood: calm, confident, considered, evidence-led. Not futuristic, not hyped.
Palette: deep navy #102b4a, ink #102846, cool paper white #f6f8fa, one accent blue #2166ac,
sky blue #a7d8ff on dark. Cool, soft light. No neon, no purple, no saturated orange.
Setting: real cities, modern uncluttered workplaces, and civic buildings.
Camera: steady, slow. Locked-off or very slow push-in. Natural depth of field.
Avoid: glowing brains, robots, humanoid AI, circuit boards, binary code, data rain,
holograms, particle effects, lens flares, staged handshakes, people pointing at screens,
text or logos in the image.
```

## Shot prompts

**Establishing city shot (dark scene, text on the left).**

```text
Slow push-in on a major city skyline at blue hour, seen from across a river.
Calm water, soft cool light, lit office windows. Composition weighted to the right third,
with the left half quiet and dark enough to sit under a navy gradient for a headline.
16:9, 1920x1080, 6 seconds, no text, no people close to camera.
```

**Modern workplace (light scene).**

```text
A bright, modern open-plan office in a restored industrial building. Natural daylight,
pale wood and white walls, plants. Two or three people in quiet conversation at a table,
seen from a distance, unposed. Very slow lateral drift. Cool, neutral grade.
Clear space in the upper left for text. 16:9, 6 seconds, no screens with readable text.
```

**Civic building setting.**

```text
Early morning in a civic building atrium. Stone and glass, soft daylight,
a few people crossing in the distance. Locked-off camera, gentle movement of light.
Calm and orderly. 16:9, 6 seconds. No flags, signage or readable text.
```

**Abstract background (instead of a gradient).**

```text
A slow, soft-focus gradient of deep navy (#0c1c2c) to mid blue (#2166ac), with a faint
radial glow in the upper right. Barely perceptible movement, like light through frosted
glass. No shapes, particles or patterns. Seamless loop, 16:9, 10 seconds.
```

## Still images

```text
[Subject] photographed in [real location], editorial documentary style,
natural cool light, muted palette of navy, slate and off-white with a single blue accent.
Shallow depth of field, uncluttered background, generous negative space on the [left/right]
for a headline. No text, no logos, no AI clichés. 3:2, high resolution.
```

## Narration

For OpenAI text-to-speech, use the `onyx` voice and these instructions:

```text
Speak with a natural, educated British accent (southern English, received pronunciation).
Calm, confident, warm corporate narrator. Measured pace. Say '{{name}}' as '{{nameSpoken}}'.
```

For other voice tools, describe: “Deep male voice, educated southern English accent, calm and warm, measured pace, no dramatic emphasis.” Write numbers and web addresses as words.

## A whole film, for an agent

Use this when you ask an AI coding agent to build a film like the reference.

```text
Build a [length]-second {{name}} film at 1920x1080, 30fps, with burned-in captions.
Follow the {{name}} brand guide: tokens from /ds/tokens, type Source Sans and Source Serif,
headlines weight 400 with a serif italic emphasis in blue (sky on dark).
Scenes alternate light and dark: photography under a navy scrim, white, or brand gradients.
One idea per scene, one line of narration per scene, cue each element on a spoken word.
Every frame must be a pure function of time: one seeked GSAP timeline, no CSS transitions,
no Math.random or Date.now. Captions: max 7 words, 42px semibold white on rgba(12,28,48,.84),
54px from the bottom; keep the bottom 150px clear.
End on the end-card gradient with "Book a conversation" and {{domain}}, then a credits page.
Every claim must be supported by {{domain}} or the published essays.
Credit every photographer and disclose the generated voice.
Script:
[scene-by-scene lines]
```
