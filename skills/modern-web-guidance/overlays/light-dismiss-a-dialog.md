In a React app, do not hand-write this control. Keep the shadcn `dialog` from the component map in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/kb/ui-components.md` and its own dismiss. Check it against this guide: a visible close button, an accessible name, an inner combobox or menu that handles Escape without closing the dialog, and important logic in `onOpenChange`, not in click handlers. A light dismiss on a destructive confirmation cancels; it never confirms.
