Do not stagger the first render of a list: our motion rules say nothing animates on first paint. Stagger only items added after load, 30–80 ms per item. Cap the total delay at about 300 ms (for example 5 × 60 ms), so a long list does not hold the reader; past the cap, the items fade in as one. Use `both` in the `animation` shorthand, so an item stays hidden during its delay. Keep `animation: none` under reduced motion.
